4.2 Kinetic Energy

حرکی توانائی (توانائی بالحرکت)
edit_note Standard Definition BISE Peshawar Target

Kinetic energy is the energy possessed by an object due to its motion. Any moving object can do work against a resisting force before coming to rest.

Practical Illustration • روزمرہ زندگی کی مثال

A fully loaded truck moving at 60 km/h possesses greater kinetic energy than a light car at the same speed due to larger mass.

functions Mathematical Derivation of K.E Formula

Consider a body of mass m initially at rest (vi = 0). When a constant external force F acts upon it through displacement s, it produces an acceleration a and acquires velocity v.

1. Work done by the force on the body: W = F × s
2. According to Newton's 2nd Law of Motion: F = m × a
3. From the 3rd Equation of Motion: 2as = vf2 − vi2
Substituting vi = 0 and vf = v: s = v2 / (2a)

Substituting the values of F and s into the work equation:

Work Done = Kinetic Energy
K.E = ½ m v²
Standard SI Unit Joule (J) 1 J = 1 kg·m²/s² = 1 N·m
